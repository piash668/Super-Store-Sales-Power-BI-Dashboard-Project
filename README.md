# Super-Store-Sales-Power-BI-Dashboard-Project
An end-to-end business intelligence solution built using Power BI to analyze retail performance, sales trends, profitability, and customer segments across the United States.  ---


Project Overview
This project transforms raw retail transaction data into an interactive, multi-page executive dashboard. It provides deep-dive insights into regional sales performance, category-level profitability, shipping preferences, and payment trends, empowering stakeholders to make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, DAX measures, interactive dashboard design, and forecasting.
* **Power Query Editor:** Data cleaning, processing, handling mixed date formats, and schema transformation.
* **Data Source:** Superstore Retail Sales Dataset (CSV).

---

## 📈 Key Dashboard Features & Components
1. **Executive Summary KPI Cards:** Real-time tracking of Total Sales ($1.57M), Total Profit ($175.26K), Total Quantity, Average Profit, and Average Delivery Time.
2. **Geographical & Regional Analysis:** Interactive maps and regional drill-downs (Central, East, South, West) highlighting state-wise sales distribution (e.g., Texas and Washington leading sales).
3. **Temporal Trend Analysis:** Line and area charts tracking monthly and yearly sum of sales and profit fluctuations.
4. **Customer Segmentation & Payment Behavior:** Breakdown of revenue contribution by consumer segments and digital/offline payment modes.
5. **Product Category & Sub-Category Insights:** Granular evaluation showing top-performing categories (Office Supplies, Technology, Furniture) and sub-categories (Phones, Chairs).
6. **Sales Forecasting:** Advanced time-series forecasting to project upcoming sales performance and demand trends.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* **Revenue Drivers:** Technology and Office Supplies generate the highest transaction volumes, with Phones and Chairs leading sub-categories.
* **Geographical Concentration:** The West and East regions lead overall sales volume, with individual state spikes observed in Texas and Washington.
* **Fulfillment Efficiency:** Standard Class remains the dominant shipping mode, with an average order-to-delivery window of approximately 3.9 days.
* **Seasonal Trends:** Sales exhibit strong upward momentum towards Q4 (October–December), reflecting holiday shopping surges.
